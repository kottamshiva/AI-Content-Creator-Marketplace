# Hackathon Demo — Creatorly

## Slide 1 — Problem
Brands cannot easily assess AI-native creative talent because traditional marketplaces hide tools, workflows, usage terms and generation-specific expertise.

## Slide 2 — Solution
Creatorly makes AI creativity legible: searchable creator profiles, AI portfolio context, visible workflow signals and structured brand briefs.

## Slide 3 — Product walkthrough
1. Search creators by skill, tool, specialty or aspect ratio.
2. Open a profile and review tools, workflow, commercial-use status and concept portfolio.
3. Enter a rough idea in the brief builder and generate an editable brief draft.
4. Publish the brief and see it persist in the workspace.

## Slide 4 — Data model and trust
Creator: identity, specialization, skills, tools/models, formats, workflow, rate, commercial terms and evidence-backed verification.
Brief: brand, objective/description, content type, style, aspect ratio, usage, budget, deadline and timestamps.
Current badges are prototype placeholders; production verification should link to evidence and reviewer status.

## 90-second demo script
“Creatorly is a marketplace built for AI-native creative work. I can search not just by job title, but by the skills and tools behind the work. Each creator profile shows the portfolio direction, generation tools, workflow, output formats and commercial-use status. On the brand side, the brief builder turns a rough idea into a structured starting draft, which can be edited before publishing. The brief captures style, format, usage rights, budget and timeline. The prototype stores briefs in SQLite. Our next step would be real creator accounts, evidence-based verification, and a hosted AI model for brief refinement.”
